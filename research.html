Overview

I am working on the topic of health and science communication in digital and emerging media, because I want to find out how different ways of presenting information—such as framing, visuals, or source type—affect audience understanding, trust, and decision-making so that I can help others to understand how to design clearer, more trustworthy messages that improve public engagement with complex or uncertain science and health topics .
