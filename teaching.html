Teaching Philosophy

My teaching focuses on helping students connect communication theory with professional practice. I emphasize critical thinking, research, strategic decision-making, and hands-on learning so that students can apply what they learn to real communication problems.

Courses

PRE 2200 — Introduction to Public Relations

An introduction to the principles, practices, and professional roles of public relations.

PRE 3600 — Social Media for Public Relations

Examines the strategic use of social media for organizations, campaigns, branding, and public engagement.

PRE 4090 — Public Relations Case Studies

Uses real-world cases to examine strategic communication decisions, challenges, and outcomes.

PRE 4600 — Public Relations Campaigns

A capstone experience in which students develop research-based strategic communication campaigns.

MCM 4360 — Mass Communication Theory and Research

Introduces major theories and research methods used in mass communication scholarship.

PRE 3450 — Publication Design

Introduces principles of visual communication and publication design.
