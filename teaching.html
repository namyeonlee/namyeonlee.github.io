My teaching focuses on connecting communication theory with professional practice through research, strategic thinking, and hands-on learning.

I want students to leave the classroom not only understanding how communication works, but also knowing how to use that knowledge to solve real communication problems.

“The real problem of humanity is the following: we have Paleolithic emotions, medieval institutions, and godlike technology.” — E. O. Wilson

current courses

Introduction to Public Relations PRE 2200

An introduction to the principles, practices, ethics, and professional roles of public relations.

Social Media for Public Relations PRE 3600

Explores the strategic use of social media for organizations, campaigns, branding, content creation, and public engagement.

Mass Communication Theory and Research MCM 4360

Introduces major mass communication theories and research methods, with an emphasis on understanding and evaluating communication research.

past courses

Public Relations Case Studies PRE 4090

Uses real-world cases to examine public relations problems, strategic decisions, ethical issues, and campaign outcomes.

Public Relations Campaigns PRE 4600

A capstone course in which students work with real clients to develop research-based public relations campaigns. In a recent project, students partnered with Hope Alive, a local nonprofit, to develop a health communication campaign addressing substance use.

Publication Design PRE 3450

Covers principles of visual communication, layout, typography, and publication design.
